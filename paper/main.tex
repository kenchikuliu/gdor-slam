\begin{abstract}
Dynamic masks protect a persistent Gaussian map, but hard exclusion can also remove background and camera-consistent observations that remain useful for localization.
This paper studies a task mismatch in dynamic RGB-D Gaussian SLAM: an observation that is useful for transient camera tracking is not necessarily eligible to modify a static world model.
We present \textbf{GDOR-SLAM}, which implements separate tracking-support and persistent-mapping routes for dynamic-region evidence.
Temporal depth consistency, residual-flow consistency, and tracking-risk state qualify a conservative subset of excluded observations for current-frame feature extraction and pose estimation, while the original conservative mask continues to govern ORB landmark insertion and Gaussian initialization, optimization, and densification.
The method therefore recovers tracking support without granting recovered dynamic-region observations persistent map authority.
The completed DYN-19 release contains a 90-run main comparison over ten sequences and three seeds, a 120-run ordered ablation, and a 72-cell multi-seed mapping diagnostic; earlier frozen DYN-15 and DYN-18 campaigns provide complementary validation.
Against semantic hard exclusion in DYN-19, GDOR lowers mean ATE from 10.119 cm to 4.930 cm (51.3\%); the paired-cell median gain is 0.213 cm, the win/tie/loss count is 19/0/11, and the largest failure-case rescue is Bonn crowd2 at 42.500 cm to 11.080 cm.
Against the map-matched no-track-reuse control, the mean decreases from 7.392 cm to 4.930 cm, but the paired-cell median gain is only 0.016 cm with a 16/0/14 record and a 4/0/6 record over sequence means; this aggregate is driven by sitting\_halfsphere at 36.775 cm to 4.864 cm.
All 30 GDOR cells pass the recovered-support overlap certificate, covering 79.498M audited pixels with no observed map-admission overlap under the declared route invariant.
This certificate establishes route behavior, not zero ghost contamination; the ordered ablation does not identify independent component effects or interactions, and protocol-matched external-baseline and independent ghost/completeness claims remain pending.
\end{abstract}
